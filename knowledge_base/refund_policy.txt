OLA SUPPORT - REFUND AND COMPENSATION POLICY

Customers may request a refund when they experience eligible payment failures, duplicate deductions, or incorrect charges. Every refund request must be verified using the available transaction and support-ticket information. Approved refunds should be processed through the original payment method whenever possible. Refund processing time depends on the payment provider and banking system. Compensation may be considered when a verified service failure causes significant inconvenience to a customer.
