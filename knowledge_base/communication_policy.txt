OLA SUPPORT - CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION POLICY

Customer communication should remain professional, respectful, clear, and helpful. Support teams may communicate with customers through approved channels such as registered email, in-app support, and official customer support contact channels. Customers should receive updates when their tickets are created, updated, escalated, or resolved. Support employees must never request passwords, OTPs, or complete payment credentials from customers. All communication should follow customer privacy and data protection guidelines.
