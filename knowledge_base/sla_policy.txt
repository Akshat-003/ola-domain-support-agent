OLA SUPPORT - SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT POLICY

The Service Level Agreement (SLA) defines the expected response and resolution time for customer support tickets. Critical tickets should receive an initial response within 15 minutes and a resolution target of 4 hours. High-priority tickets should receive a response within 1 hour and a resolution target of 12 hours. Medium-priority tickets have a response target of 4 hours and a resolution target of 24 hours, while Low-priority tickets have a response target of 24 hours and a resolution target of 72 hours. These timelines are service targets and may change depending on the complexity of the issue.
